Stop 1: European explorers

Why did countries like Spain and France send explorers to Texas? There were three big reasons.

💰

Wealth and trade

Countries wanted riches and new things to trade. These are economic opportunities.

🏁

Competition

Countries raced each other. If Spain didn’t claim a place, France might.

🗺️

Expansion

Countries wanted more power and more land. Claiming more land is called expansion.

Primary or secondary source?

Primary source

Made at the time of the event, or by someone who was there. Examples: a journal, a letter, a map drawn on an expedition, an artifact.

Secondary source

Made later to explain the past. Examples: a textbook, a historian’s summary, an encyclopedia, a website about history.

Stop 2: Spanish missions and settlements

Spain built Catholic missions in Texas. A mission was a religious settlement. Spain had two big goals for them:

  • Spread the Catholic faith.
  • Support Spanish settlement and Spain’s power in Texas.

Mission Ysleta

Recognized as the first mission established in Texas (1682, near today’s El Paso).

San Antonio missions

Important historic landmarks. One of them is the Alamo.

José de Escandón

Helped set up settlements in the lower Rio Grande Valley in the 1700s.

Why build near water?

Settlements often grew near rivers because people needed water for…

🥤Drinking
🌽Farming
🐴Animals
🛶Travel

Cultural interaction

When Spanish settlers and American Indian peoples met, they influenced each other. They exchanged knowledge, resources, crops, animals, and ways of doing things. This helped shape Texas culture.

Spanish settlers brought

Cattle, horses, sheep, ranching skills, the Spanish language, and the Catholic faith.

American Indian peoples shared

Knowledge of the land, water, and plants, crops like corn, beans, and squash, and trade routes.

Not everyone experienced the missions the same way. Some American Indian people came to missions for food and protection. Others did not want to change their ways of life. New diseases also made many people sick.

Stop 3: Mexican independence and new settlers

1821: Mexico wins independence from Spain

Texas was now part of Mexico. A new government meant new rules about who could settle in Texas.

Mexico let empresarios bring settlers. An empresario was a person given permission or a land grant to bring settlers to Texas and start a colony.

Stephen F. Austin

The most famous empresario. He brought hundreds of Anglo-American families to Texas and set up Austin’s Colony.

Martín De León

Another empresario. He was a Tejano (a Texan of Mexican heritage) who founded the town of Victoria.

Why did settlers come?

🏞️Get land
🌾Farm
🤝Trade
🏡Build a new life

Farming and raising livestock (cows, pigs, horses) were important jobs for many early settlers.
