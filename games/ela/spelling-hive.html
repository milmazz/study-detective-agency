Honey’s Spelling Hive

Honey the bee is getting you ready for the class spelling bee! Study the words, practice with Honey reading them out loud, then try Bee Day, a dress rehearsal for the real thing.

🔇 This browser can’t read words out loud, so Honey can’t talk here. Try Safari or Chrome, or ask a grown-up to read the words to you. In Practice there’s a 👀 Peek button that flashes the word for 2 seconds.

How a spelling bee works

1. Listen

The pronouncer says your word. Listen to every sound.

2. Ask

You can ask to hear it again, or for the definition, a sentence, the part of speech, or the language of origin.

3. Spell

Say the word, spell it one letter at a time, then say the word again.

🐝Honey’s tip: when you practice, say each letter out loud as you type it. That’s exactly what you’ll do at the bee!
